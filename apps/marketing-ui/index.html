What kind of replies does Syntrae generate?

Can operators review or edit replies before sending?

Yes. Pending Replies supports review, editing, approval, and thread-target visibility before posting.

Does Syntrae only work for one brand?

No. The platform is brand-scoped so each workspace can manage distinct brands, sessions, and reply context safely.

Can Syntrae support TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube workflows too?

Syntrae is currently focused on Xiaohongshu and Rednote performance, with expansion paths for broader social media comment lead generation and AI reply workflows.
